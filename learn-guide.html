Spicy Snack Buying Guide

What to try first, how to save with bundles, and how to keep your shelf stocked and fresh.

Start here: first-time buyers should open with the First-Timer Variety Pack — it spans Heat 1–5 in small portions, so you find your spice ceiling before committing to a full case. Bundles save about 18% versus single prices, and orders over $35 ship free from our US warehouse in 1 business day.

Start with a variety pack

If you've never ordered from us, the First-Timer Variety Pack is the smart move. It spans Heat 1 to 5 in small portions, so you find your spice ceiling before committing to a full case of one snack. It also makes a great shared box for a group with mixed heat tolerances.

Build around strips and bites

Our range splits into two easy lanes. Spicy strips (mala latiao) are the savory, chewy, numbing centerpiece — start at Heat 4 if you like heat, Heat 3 if you're cautious. Chocolate bites (planet cups, wafer rolls, coffee candy) are the sweet reset between spicy rounds, and they're no-heat, so they please everyone.

Save with bundles

Buying a few things separately adds up in shipping and per-item cost. The Party Bundle bundles strips and bites at about 18% off single prices and qualifies for free shipping over $35. For regular snackers, a bundle per month beats reordering singles.

Gifting

A variety pack or bundle is an easy, low-risk gift for a friend who says they "like spicy food" — they can self-sort the heat. Add a note at checkout (contact us if you need help), and it ships in one business day, so it works for last-minute occasions.

Storage

Most items are shelf-stable for 6–9 months unopened, no refrigeration. Keep them sealed, cool, and out of sunlight. Once opened, reseal the pack — the strips stay chewy for a while but taste best fresh. Check each product page for exact shelf life.
