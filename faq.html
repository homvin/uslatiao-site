Frequently asked

How fast do you ship?

From our US warehouse. Orders before 2pm ET go out the same business day; most addresses get it in 2–4 business days. Free over $35.

Where are these made?

The snacks are produced by established Asian food makers. We import and stock them in the US so you don't wait on ocean freight.

I'm nervous about spice. Where do I start?

The First-Timer Variety Pack spans Heat 1 to 5. Start low, work up. If hot Cheetos are your ceiling, begin with the mild end.

What about allergens?

Every product page lists ingredients and contains-statements (wheat, soy, milk, sesame, etc.). Read before you reorder.

What payment methods do you accept?

Stripe (all major cards) and PayPal. Checkout is a secure Stripe-hosted page — your card is charged when the order is placed, and it ships from our US warehouse within 1 business day.

Can I return food?

Unopened within 30 days, yes. See the Shipping & Returns page.
