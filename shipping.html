Shipping & Returns

The short version: we're already in the US, so this is fast and simple.

Shipping

  • Handling: orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day.
  • Transit: most US addresses arrive in 2–4 business days via ground.
  • Free shipping: on orders over $35.
  • Flat rate: $4.99 below $35.
  • Where: contiguous US only for now. Alaska, Hawaii and international coming later.

Returns

  • Not your taste? Return unopened items within 30 days.
  • Email us first so we can set up the return — no RMA roulette.
  • Refunds go back to your original method within 5 business days of receipt.
  • Opened food can't be resold, so it's not returnable — but tell us if something's off.
